ZLOON policies

Shopping with confidence.

Order confirmation

Style Bag requests are confirmed by the ZLOON team after size, colour and availability are checked. The final payable amount is shared before dispatch.

Delivery

Delivery locations, timelines and charges are confirmed by WhatsApp before an order is accepted.

Exchange and returns

Return and exchange eligibility will be confirmed for each order before dispatch. Please keep the item unused with original tags and packaging.

Privacy

ZLOON uses customer name, email, phone and delivery details only to manage membership, orders, bookings and customer support. We do not sell customer data.
